Definitions and data notes

  • Segments (business rules): Premium = unit price at or above the median (); Bulk = 6 to 10 units per order; Light = 1 to 5 units.
  • AOV = revenue / orders. MoM growth = (last full month - previous month) / previous month within the selected range; January 2026 is excluded as a partial month. Top-20% concentration = revenue of the largest 20% of orders / total revenue.
  • 13 orders have an unknown city. 20 orders had a missing age filled in Task 1, so they appear as "Unknown" in the age filter.
  • The dataset behaves like simulated data (flat distributions, wide price ranges for every product), so treat the patterns as a demonstration of method.
